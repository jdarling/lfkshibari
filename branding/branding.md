# LFKShibari Branding Guidelines

## Naming

The community name is `LFKShibari`.

When the name appears inline or on a single line, always use `LFKShibari` with no space.

When the name is intentionally broken across multiple lines or used as a stacked visual treatment, use:

`LFK`  
`Shibari`

`LFK` and `Shibari` are always capitalized exactly as shown.

## Usage

- `LFKShibari`: community name in normal written and inline usage
- `LFK Shibari`: stacked or multi-line visual usage only
- `lfkshibari`: URLs, domain names, file paths, service names, social handles, and technical identifiers where lowercase is required or preferred

Do not use `LFKshibari`, `LfkShibari`, `LFK SHIBARI`, or other capitalization variants in normal brand usage.

## URLs and Domain Names

All URLs, domain names, file paths, and technical identifiers should use lowercase as a standard web convention.

## Mission Statement

To create a Lawrence-area rope community where people can learn, practice, connect, and grow around shibari, kinbaku, rope bondage, and rope-adjacent practice with a focus on education, consent, mutual respect, and responsible risk-aware practice.

## Vision Statement

To build a sustainable local rope community where people of different roles and experience levels can participate, learn, and connect while maintaining clear expectations around consent, privacy, accountability, and risk.

## Community Position

- LFKShibari is a Lawrence, Kansas rope community centered on education, consent, connection, and responsible practice.
- LFKShibari welcomes different roles, experience levels, bodies, identities, and approaches to rope.
- Community trust is built through behavior, accountability, and continued respect for consent. Vetting or admission is not a guarantee that any individual is safe.

## Brand Voice

- Direct
- Welcoming
- Respectful
- Risk-aware
- Community-centered

## Main Purpose

- Create opportunities for people in Lawrence and the surrounding area to learn, practice, connect, and grow around rope.
- Support both newcomers and experienced practitioners without requiring participation beyond each person's own interests and boundaries.

## Why This Matters

- Rope communities work best when expectations around consent, privacy, skill, accountability, and risk are clear before problems occur.
- Newcomers should be able to enter the community without pressure to tie, be tied, or already know everything.

## Desired Impact

- Build a strong and sustainable local rope community.
- Make education, consent, privacy, accountability, and responsible risk awareness part of normal community practice.
- Create a space where people can learn, observe, socialize, self-tie, tie, or be tied without pressure to participate beyond their own boundaries.

## Color System

- Background Black: `#000000`
- Primary Green: `#2f743e`
- White: `#ffffff`

Supporting website colors may be used when needed for interaction and readability:

- Green Hover/Accent: `#3e9150`
- Near Black: `#0b0b0b`
- Off White: `#f4f4f1`

## Color Usage

- Black is the primary background color and should dominate branded layouts.
- Green is the primary brand color and should be used for `LFK`, links, primary buttons, section accents, highlights, and interactive states.
- White is the primary text color on black backgrounds and should also be used for `Shibari`, callouts, borders, and high-contrast elements.
- Light backgrounds may be used sparingly for content sections where improved readability is useful.
- Avoid introducing additional strong brand colors unless there is a specific functional need.

## Typography

- <a href="https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Work+Sans" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Work Sans Ultra-Bold</a>: logo font for `LFK`; available free through Google Fonts
- `Anton`: website display font for large headings and strong section titles
- <a href="https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alex+Brush" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Alex Brush</a>: script font for `Shibari` in the logo and branded decorative treatments; available free through Google Fonts
- `Inter`: primary body and interface font for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, forms, and general website content

`Alex Brush` should remain an accent font and should not be used for body copy or long passages.

## Logo Color Rules

- Primary logo treatment:
  - Black background
  - `LFK` in Primary Green (`#2f743e`)
  - `Shibari` in White (`#ffffff`)
- The logo should be given enough clear space that the script remains legible.
- Do not recolor `LFK` or `Shibari` with unrelated brand colors.
- On light backgrounds, use a logo variant that maintains strong contrast and preserves the relationship between green `LFK` and the `Shibari` wordmark.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or independently reposition portions of the logo outside an approved alternate layout.

## Wordmark Rules

- Inline naming: `LFKShibari`
- Multi-line or stacked naming:
  - `LFK`
  - `Shibari`
- `LFK` should use Work Sans Ultra-Bold when presented as a visual wordmark.
- `Shibari` should use Alex Brush when presented as part of the visual wordmark.
- Written references in body copy should use the normal body font rather than recreating the logo typography inline.

## Website Style

- Default page background is black.
- Navigation and major dark sections use black or near-black backgrounds.
- Green is used for links, primary actions, eyebrows, highlights, and branded emphasis.
- White is used for primary text, callouts, outlines, and contrast.
- Large headings may use Anton in uppercase.
- Body content should remain simple, readable, and direct using Inter.
- Decorative use of Alex Brush should be limited to the `Shibari` wordmark and occasional brand accents.

## Core Messaging Themes

LFKShibari content should consistently reinforce:

- Education
- Consent
- Connection
- Mutual respect
- Privacy
- Accountability
- Honest representation of skill and experience
- Responsible, risk-aware rope practice
- Respect for newcomers and individual boundaries

## Logo Asset Files

Preferred logo assets should include:

- `lfkshibari-logo.svg`: primary full logo for dark backgrounds
- Light-background logo variant when required
- Compact mark or simplified treatment for favicon, social avatar, or small-format use if one is created
