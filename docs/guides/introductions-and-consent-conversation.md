# Introductions and Consent Conversation

Use this as a conversation guide to establish a shared baseline of comfort and compatibility.

This conversation does not need to happen at an event or immediately before tying. Meeting for coffee, a light meal, or another casual setting can give both people space to talk without the pressure or expectation of a scene afterward.

The goal is to learn enough about each other to make informed choices about whether and how you might want to tie together. This is a conversation, not an interview, and either person can ask questions, add context, change their mind, or decide they are not interested in tying together.

---

## Introductions

Start by introducing yourselves before talking about the scene. The examples below are only prompts. Use whatever language feels natural.

### Rigger

**Example introduction**

> Hi, I'm ________. I've been tying for about ________. I usually enjoy or focus on ________. My experience with the kinds of rope I enjoy or practice is ________. I want you to feel comfortable asking questions, sharing concerns, or saying something is not for you at any point.

You may also want to share:

- What name would you like me to use?
- What pronouns do you use?
- How long have you been tying?
- How often do you currently tie?
- What kinds of rope do you most enjoy?
- What kinds of ties are you most comfortable with?
- What kinds of ties are you less comfortable with?
- Are there techniques you are still learning or practicing?
- How comfortable are you with:
  - Floor work?
  - Partial suspension?
  - Full suspension?
  - Dynamic movement?
  - Restrictive ties?
  - Longer scenes?
- Are there things you do not tie or generally prefer not to tie?

### Bunny

**Example introduction**

> Hi, I'm ________. I've been getting tied for about ________. The kinds of rope experiences I've had include ________. I generally enjoy ________, and things I know I am less comfortable with are ________. I'll tell you if something changes, feels wrong, or I want to stop.

You may also want to share:

- What name would you like me to use?
- What pronouns do you use?
- How long have you been getting tied?
- How often do you currently get tied?
- What kinds of rope experiences have you had?
- What kinds of ties or positions are you most comfortable with?
- What kinds of ties or positions are you less comfortable with?
- How comfortable are you with:
  - Floor work?
  - Partial suspension?
  - Full suspension?
  - Dynamic movement?
  - Restrictive ties?
  - Longer scenes?
- Are there things you generally do not want to do in rope?

## Injuries and Physical Considerations

### Questions for Both People

- Do you have any injuries I should know about?
- Do you have any old injuries that can become painful or aggravated?
- Do you have any joint or mobility limitations?
- Are there positions that are difficult or uncomfortable for you?
- Are there areas that are especially sensitive to pressure?
- Do you have any existing numbness, tingling, weakness, or nerve issues?
- Do you have any bruises, cuts, recent piercings, skin irritation, or other areas we should avoid?
- Are there physical considerations that tend to vary from day to day or that would be important to discuss before tying?
- Are there temporary conditions, such as fatigue, soreness, illness, stress, hunger, or dehydration, that you would want to account for before tying?

---

## Touch Without Rope

- Where are you comfortable being touched?
- Where are you not comfortable being touched?
- Are there areas where incidental contact is okay, but deliberate touch is not?
- Are there areas where you want me to ask before touching?
- Are you comfortable with me helping position your body?
- Are you comfortable with me supporting, lifting, catching, or moving your body?
- Are there types of touch you generally do not want?
- Is there any touch that may be surprising or uncomfortable even if it is not sexual or intimate?

---

## Touch With Rope

- Where are you comfortable having rope placed?
- Where do you not want rope placed?
- Are there areas where rope can cross, but should not apply pressure?
- Are there areas where rope pressure is especially uncomfortable?
- Are there areas where rope pressure feels particularly good?
- Are you comfortable with rope directly on skin?
- Are you comfortable with rope being used to move or position your body?
- Are there ties, positions, or forms of restriction you do not want?

---

## Communication

- How do you prefer to communicate discomfort?
- Do you prefer frequent check-ins or fewer interruptions?
- Do you tend to become quiet when you are focused, uncomfortable, or overwhelmed?
- Are there signs I should watch for if you are having trouble communicating?
- What word or signal should mean:
  - Slow down?
  - Change something?
  - Stop what we are doing?
  - End the scene?
- Are you comfortable speaking up if something does not feel right?
- Are you comfortable stopping the scene without needing to explain why?
- Is there anything that makes it harder for you to speak up in the moment?

---

## Boundaries

- What are your hard limits?
- What are your softer limits or things you may be unsure about?
- Is there anything you only want to do after checking in first?
- Is there anything you definitely do not want in a rope scene?
- Are there things you enjoy that you would like me to know about?
- Are there things you have had a bad experience with before?
- Are there words, behaviors, or dynamics you do not want included?
- Are you comfortable with photography or video?
- If yes, what is okay to photograph, keep, or share?

---

## Interests and Intentions

- What kinds of experiences do you enjoy or want to explore?
- What kinds of things are you interested in exploring?
- Are you looking for:
  - Practice?
  - Learning?
  - Connection?
  - Challenge?
  - Aesthetics?
  - Sensation?
  - Restraint?
  - Stillness?
  - Movement?
  - Something else?
- How intense do you want the scene to feel?
- Is there anything new you are interested in trying?
- Are there things you are not interested in trying?
- Are there specific ties or positions you especially enjoy or want to explore?
- Are there specific ties or positions you prefer to avoid?
- What tends to make a rope experience feel good or worthwhile for you?

---

## Ending and Aftercare

- How do you usually feel when coming out of rope?
- Do you want help getting up or moving afterward?
- Do you want touch after the scene?
- Do you prefer space after the scene?
- Would you like water, food, quiet, conversation, or something else?
- Is there anything you would like me to check once the rope is off?
- Would you like to check in later after the scene?

---

## Wrap-up Questions

- Is there anything I have not asked that you think I should know?
- Is there anything you are unsure about?
- Is there anything you want to ask me before we start?
- Has anything come up during this conversation that changes how you feel about possibly tying together?
- Do you feel like we understand each other's expectations and boundaries?
- Is there interest in continuing the conversation or potentially tying together in the future?
