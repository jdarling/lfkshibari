# Rigger Agreement

This document is a guide to what you can expect when you interact with me. It is a work in progress and may change as I learn and grow.

It is intended to communicate how I approach rope, consent, communication, safety, boundaries, and the responsibility I take on when tying with someone.

This is not a substitute for conversation. Every person, scene, and situation is different, and anything written here can be discussed, clarified, changed, or declined.

## General Standards

- I will be honest about my experience, abilities, limitations, and intentions.
- I will respect your boundaries without requiring you to justify them.
- Agreeing to rope does not imply agreement to sensual, sexual, romantic, or other activity.
- Previous consent does not imply current consent.
- Either of us may decide not to begin or may stop at any time.
- Unless we have an established tying relationship, I generally prefer to tie in public, community, class, event, or other mutually comfortable settings.

## Scene Intention

I generally think about rope in three ways:

- **Rope as the Activity:** Rope, practice, learning, teaching, restraint, movement, aesthetics, sensation, or challenge are the focus. Touch is limited to what is reasonably related to the activity unless otherwise discussed.
- **Rope as Connection:** The scene may intentionally include closeness, affection, sensuality, or other forms of connection. This should be discussed before we begin.
- **Rope Involving Sexual Contact:** Kissing, sexual touch, or other sexual activity will always be specifically discussed and negotiated beforehand.

Consent to rope is not consent to sex or other intimate activity.

## Consent and Communication

- I will only proceed after we have discussed what we intend to do and both agree to it.
- Consent may be changed or withdrawn at any time, including after tying has begun.
- We will agree on how to communicate slow down, change something, stop, or end the scene.
- You do not need to explain why you want something changed or stopped.
- If you ask to be untied, I will begin getting you out of the rope and will cut rope if necessary.
- I will pay attention to both verbal and non-verbal communication.

## What I Ask From Rope Partners

- Tell me about injuries, mobility limitations, nerve concerns, sensitive areas, or other physical considerations that may affect the scene.
- Tell me about anything that may affect awareness, judgment, pain perception, balance, or communication.
- Tell me if something changes or feels wrong while we are tying.
- You do not owe me your complete medical history. I need enough information to make informed decisions about how we tie.

## The Standards I Hold Myself To as a Rigger

- I will be honest if something is new, experimental, or something I am still learning.
- I will only use rope and equipment that I consider appropriate and in suitable condition.
- I will keep a safety cutter immediately accessible and show you where it is before we begin.
- When you are in my rope, you have my attention.
- I may decline or stop a tie, technique, suspension, transition, or scene if I do not believe I can manage it safely.
- The scene we discussed is a plan, not an obligation to complete everything we talked about.

## After the Scene

- I will check in with you once the rope is off.
- I will ask rather than assume what kind of aftercare you want.
- When appropriate, I may check in later to see how you are doing.
- I welcome feedback about what worked, what did not, or anything you would want handled differently.

## Photography and Privacy

- Consent to rope does not include consent to photography or recording.
- We will separately discuss whether photos or video may be taken, kept, shared, or posted.
- Permission to take a photo does not automatically include permission to share or post it.
- I will respect the privacy of personal information shared during negotiation.
- Venue or event photography policies may add additional restrictions.

## Identity and Age Verification

- I only engage in adult rope and BDSM activities with adults.
- If age verification is appropriate, I may ask to visually inspect a valid government-issued photo ID.
- I will not photograph, copy, scan, record, or retain your identification.
- You may ask to inspect mine as well.

## Accountability

I accept responsibility for communicating honestly, respecting consent and boundaries, working within my abilities, maintaining my equipment, paying attention while tying, responding when something changes, listening to feedback, and learning from mistakes.

No rope practice is without risk. My responsibility is to understand those risks as well as I reasonably can, communicate honestly, pay attention, and respond when circumstances change.
