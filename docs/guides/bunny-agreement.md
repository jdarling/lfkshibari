# Bunny Agreement

This document is a guide to what you can expect when you interact with me. It is a work in progress and may change as I learn and grow.

It is intended to communicate how I approach rope, consent, communication, safety, boundaries, and the responsibility I take on when being tied.

This is not a substitute for conversation. Every person, scene, and situation is different, and anything written here can be discussed, clarified, changed, or declined.

## General Standards

- I will be honest about my experience, abilities, limitations, and intentions.
- I will respect your boundaries without requiring you to justify them.
- Agreeing to rope does not imply agreement to sensual, sexual, romantic, or other activity.
- Previous consent does not imply current consent.
- Either of us may decide not to begin or may stop at any time.
- Unless we have an established tying relationship, I generally prefer to tie in public, community, class, event, or other mutually comfortable settings.

## Scene Intention

I generally think about rope in three ways:

- **Rope as the Activity:** Rope, practice, learning, restraint, movement, aesthetics, sensation, or challenge are the focus.
- **Rope as Connection:** The scene may intentionally include closeness, affection, sensuality, or other forms of connection. This should be discussed before we begin.
- **Rope Involving Sexual Contact:** Kissing, sexual touch, or other sexual activity will always be specifically discussed and negotiated beforehand.

Consent to rope is not consent to sex or other intimate activity.

## Consent and Communication

- I will communicate my boundaries, limits, and expectations as clearly as I can.
- Consent may be changed or withdrawn at any time, including after tying has begun.
- We will agree on how I can communicate slow down, change something, stop, or end the scene.
- I will tell you when something feels wrong, uncomfortable, unsafe, or different from what I expected.
- I do not need to explain or justify why I want something changed or stopped.
- I will not intentionally endure a problem simply because I do not want to interrupt the scene.

## What I Ask From Riggers

- Be honest about your experience, abilities, and limitations.
- Tell me if something is new, experimental, or something you are still learning.
- Respect the boundaries and intentions we discuss.
- Keep appropriate safety equipment accessible.
- Pay attention to me while I am in your rope.
- Tell me if you need to change or stop the scene for safety or any other reason.

## The Standards I Hold Myself To as a Bunny

- I will communicate injuries, mobility limitations, nerve concerns, sensitive areas, or other physical considerations that may affect the scene.
- I will disclose anything that may materially affect my awareness, judgment, pain perception, balance, or communication.
- I will communicate changes such as numbness, tingling, weakness, unusual pain, dizziness, panic, or difficulty breathing.
- I will be honest about my experience and comfort with the ties or techniques we are considering.
- I will not intentionally misrepresent my physical or emotional condition in order to continue a scene.

## During the Scene

I understand that rope involves risk and that communication is part of managing that risk.

I will pay attention to changes in my body and communicate when something does not feel right.

I understand that the scene we discussed is a plan, not an obligation to complete everything we talked about.

Either of us can change the plan or end the scene.

## After the Scene

- I will communicate what kind of aftercare I want, if any.
- I will let you know if I notice numbness, tingling, weakness, unusual pain, mobility concerns, or anything else unexpected.
- When appropriate, I will communicate concerns that appear later.
- I will give feedback about what worked, what did not, or anything I would want handled differently.

## Photography and Privacy

- Consent to rope does not include consent to photography or recording.
- We will separately discuss whether photos or video may be taken, kept, shared, or posted.
- Permission to take a photo does not automatically include permission to share or post it.
- I will respect the privacy of personal information shared during negotiation.
- Venue or event photography policies may add additional restrictions.

## Identity and Age Verification

- I only engage in adult rope and BDSM activities with adults.
- If age verification is appropriate, I may ask to visually inspect a valid government-issued photo ID.
- I will not photograph, copy, scan, record, or retain your identification.
- You may ask to inspect mine as well.
