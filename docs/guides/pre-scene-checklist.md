# Ready-to-Tie Checklist

Use this immediately before starting a scene.

This is a quick readiness check to make sure both people, the rope, the equipment, and the space are ready before tying begins.

---

## Space

- [ ] Tying area is clear
- [ ] Floor is clear of objects, spills, trip hazards, and sharp items
- [ ] There is enough room to move safely
- [ ] Barrier or mat is in place, if needed
- [ ] Furniture or anchor points have been checked, if used
- [ ] The working area is clear of unnecessary traffic
- [ ] Exit and walkway are clear
- [ ] Lighting is good enough to see rope placement and body condition clearly

---

## Rope and Equipment

- [ ] Rope has been inspected
- [ ] Rope is clean and appropriate for use
- [ ] Damaged or questionable rope has been removed
- [ ] Cutter is accessible without searching
- [ ] Suspension hardware has been inspected, if used
- [ ] Mats, padding, or support equipment are ready, if used
- [ ] Everything we expect to need is within reach

---

## Rigger

- [ ] I am comfortable with the ties or techniques I plan to use
- [ ] I know which parts, if any, are practice or new territory
- [ ] I have a cutter immediately accessible
- [ ] I have shown where the cutter is
- [ ] My pockets are empty of anything that could cause injury
- [ ] Phone, watch, jewelry, keys, or other items are handled as needed
- [ ] I have checked the rope I plan to use

---

## Bunny

- [ ] My pockets are empty
- [ ] Jewelry and accessories are handled as needed
- [ ] My clothing works for what we plan to do
- [ ] I have mentioned any injuries, soreness, numbness, tingling, bruising, or limitations
- [ ] I know where the cutter is
- [ ] I know how I will communicate slow down, change, stop, or end
- [ ] I am comfortable with the ties and level of intensity we discussed

---

## Both People

- [ ] We have talked about what we want to do
- [ ] We have talked about what we do not want to do
- [ ] We have discussed today's intention
- [ ] We have discussed any specific ties or techniques we plan to use
- [ ] We have identified anything new or experimental
- [ ] We have discussed current injuries, soreness, or limitations
- [ ] We have confirmed touch boundaries
- [ ] We know how we will communicate slow down, change, stop, or end
- [ ] We both feel physically ready
- [ ] We both feel mentally ready
- [ ] Water, food, rest, and comfort needs are addressed
